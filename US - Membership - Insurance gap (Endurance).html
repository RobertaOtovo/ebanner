Most people assume their homeowners’ insurance covers their solar. Sometimes it does, but the details vary a lot, and the gaps are easy to miss.

 

A few things worth checking on your policy:

  • Are the panels covered for storm, hail or fire damage, or only the building they’re attached to?

  • Does it cover the inverter and wiring, or just the panels themselves?

  • Is there anything for lost production while the system is being repaired?

  • Are wear and tear or electrical faults excluded?

 

Coverage may vary by policy and by market, so the only way to know is to read yours.
